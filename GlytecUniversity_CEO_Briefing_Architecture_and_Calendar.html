The Learning Architecture

Three pillars. One system. In that order. Every time.

01 · Knowledge

Self-Paced Context

Synthesia welcome, GlytecNation podcasts (8 case study + 6 outreach episodes), persona microsites, Day 1 Field Guide, SalesSparx modules, Information Hub library.

02 · Practice

AI-Coached Reps

20 AI Discovery Practice Scenarios in Week 1 alone (5 per persona, Phase 1 + Phase 2), scored live by Claude API. Live call shadowing with Fathom debriefs.

03 · Execution

Real Accounts

Real outreach to real accounts every day, building toward Proof Point 1 (PP 1) — a mini-discovery delivered to the manager on Day 5. Logged in Salesforce, recorded in Fathom, reviewed by manager.
